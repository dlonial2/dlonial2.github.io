About

My background spans both cognitive science and computer science, which naturally led me to an interest in intelligent systems and the intersection of human decision-making and technology. I'm particularly drawn to machine learning, backend engineering, and the challenge of building software that is both technically robust and genuinely useful.

Professionally, I enjoy working across the full lifecycle of software development—from designing and implementing backend services to improving reliability, observability, and scalability in production environments. At the same time, my graduate studies in Machine Learning and AI continue to deepen my understanding of how modern intelligent systems are built, evaluated, and deployed.

What excites me most about engineering is continuous learning. Whether I'm exploring new machine learning techniques, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, or software architecture, I'm always looking to better understand how complex systems work and how they can be improved.
