About

My background is in cognitive science and computer science, so I'm drawn to where how people think meets how machines learn. These days that pulls me toward applied ML: building models that are explainable, useful, and actually deployed—not just accurate on a slide.

Through my OMSCS at Georgia Tech I'm learning ML and AI from the fundamentals up—I built a GPT from scratch in NumPy to understand the internals—and I care a lot about the production side: deployment, reliability, and how systems behave under real load.

Tech Stack:

  • Frameworks: Spring Boot, Flask, React, Node.js
  • Cloud & Infra: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD (Jenkins/Tekton), Autosys
  • Data & ML: Elasticsearch, Pandas, NumPy, REST APIs, model serving & evaluation
  • Observability & Quality: AppDynamics, Kibana, logging/metrics, SonarQube, Snyk, Swagger
